# Fieldwork QA Checklist

Use this checklist around the physical visit. The client's supplied assignment guideline takes precedence wherever it differs.

## Before the visit

- Confirm the exact city, venue, deadline and reimbursement cap.
- Confirm the BARE survey submission deadline (the post states 24 hours after the visit).
- Read the client guideline and map any mandatory questions into the local notes template.
- Confirm whether additional venue photos are permitted; if unclear, collect only the receipt evidence requested by the client.
- Decide on alcoholic vs non-alcoholic ordering. If ordering alcohol, confirm the shopper is legally eligible in the assignment jurisdiction and under client rules.
- Do not pre-fill observations or expected service outcomes.

## During the visit

- Behave as a normal guest and avoid disrupting service.
- Note arrival/departure time and approximate service wait time.
- Observe greeting, attentiveness, professionalism, cleanliness and comfort.
- Record observable facts rather than assumptions about staff motives.
- Note the menu/quoted amount before payment where possible.
- Record the charged amount and payment method.
- Ask for/retain the receipt when consistent with the assignment instructions.
- Do not create staged or fabricated evidence.
- Do not use covert audio/video recording through this toolkit.

## Immediately after the visit

- Photograph/scan the receipt clearly if required.
- Check that venue code, date, amounts and notes agree.
- Enter observations while details are fresh.
- Run the local QA gate.
- Investigate any charged-versus-receipt mismatch before submission.
- Explain missing receipt evidence rather than inventing it.
- Complete the official BARE survey within the stated client deadline.

## Evidence quality gate

Receipt evidence should be legible enough to verify, when present:

- date/time if printed;
- purchased item or transaction reference;
- total amount;
- venue identity where printed;
- payment/tax/service-charge details relevant to the survey.

Reject/reshoot a receipt image that is blurred, cropped through the total, overexposed or unreadable.
